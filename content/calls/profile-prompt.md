# Profile pass

This file is a prompt. A model runs it over the ledger and writes profile.md. The tally never depends on it, and profile.md can be deleted on its own.

To run it: in Claude Code, opened at the repo root, say `follow content/calls/profile-prompt.md`. Or from a shell at the repo root: `claude -p "$(cat content/calls/profile-prompt.md)"`.

## Instructions to the model

Read content/calls/calls.md and content/calls/readout.md. Write content/calls/profile.md and nothing else. Do not edit calls.md, readout.md or any other file. Today's date is the date you run.

The first line of profile.md is: `Generated by a model on <date>. Not Adit's words. Delete freely.` The second line names what was read and how many calls and settles there were.

Then five sections, in this order, under `##` headings. Lists only; no paragraphs of advice. A settle verb of blind means the right model was never on Adit's list; missed means it was and the wrong one was chosen. Treat both as misses below, and keep the verb visible.

Known. Per domain, what has held: compress the calls that settled held into the models and assumptions that have worked, in Adit's own words (quote the call text, do not paraphrase into your own terms), each with its ids. Where several held calls say the same thing, group them and give the count.

Weaknesses. Cluster the misses across domains into named patterns. A pattern needs at least two misses; name it in plain words taken from the settle texts or from the call texts where Adit links one miss to another (for example "dropped a loss term", "assumed steady state", "went to the first tool that fit"). For each pattern: the ids, the domains, the settle date of the last instance, and whether any later call's text cites one of its ids (applied) or none does (not applied). Misses that fit no pattern go under "once", one line each.

Dormant. Copy the two lists from readout.md's Dormant section, then add: weakness patterns with no new instance and no citation in the dormant window, named with their last date.

Questions. At most three, each answerable in one word, each about something in the ledger that could not be read (a miss whose settle text does not say what was needed, a call whose domain token looks like a typo, a citation that may point to the wrong id). Nothing else.

Drills. At most three analogies of the form `A : B :: C : ?`, built only from Adit's own lines: A is a settled call in one domain, B is what its settle text says was needed, C is an open call or a domain with recent calls in a different domain, and the question mark is left blank. Example of the shape: `c1 furnace : "radiation off the element above 900 C" :: c44 asic : ?`. Choose C where a weakness pattern from another domain has never been cited. Do not fill the blank, do not add a hint, do not say whether the analogy holds. Adit answers with a call line that cites A, and reality settles it.

Rules that override everything above: never propose a model, an assumption, a derivation, a theory or a method; never say which domain to call next or what to call; never say whether a call was right beyond what its settle line says; never add a line to calls.md. Only link, count, flag and ask. Keep profile.md under 80 lines.
