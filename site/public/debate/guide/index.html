Rebuttal Board

MIE315 · against the motion "Individual action has more impact on the environment than government policy."

Open the board

Before the round

  1. One person opens the board and presses Session, then New. The code appears after #s- in the address bar.
  2. Everyone else opens the same link, or presses Session and types the code. Four people, four devices, one log.
  3. Type your first name in the header once. Everything you log carries it.
  4. Read the Corpus tab once. The order inside each group is strongest argument first; the greyed groups at the bottom are our own frames and measured wins.

During their speech

  1. Type what they said, in their words, and press Enter. Short is fine: "vegan cuts footprint 73 percent".
  2. Three cards appear under it at once, best match first. The line in serif is the line to say aloud. Tap a card's title for the detail, the sources, and what to say if they push back.
  3. If Haiku is on, it re-orders the cards a second or two later and adds one italic linking sentence. That sentence is Haiku's phrasing, not a fact; the facts are on the cards.
  4. Press More on a point to see all matches, attach a note or link, re-rank, or remove it. Press Use this in the outline on the card you intend to run.

Building the speeches

  • Outline → Build under Rebuttal gives up to six beats for four minutes, each with a time mark, the line to say and the source to name. Everyone sees the latest build.
  • Build the Closing the same way: a frame that resets the motion, the wins that beat what they actually argued, and the line to end on. Copy gives plain text.
  • The take-away line is on card F4: Individuals choose from the menu. Government writes the menu.

Reading the marks

  • ? or check source: a source on that card was never fetched by us. Say the point, soften or skip the number.
  • weak: the best answer we have, but a judge may not buy it. Answer briefly and move on.
  • Card ids: F1–F4 frames, M1–M5 measured examples, A/B/C Adit's rebuttals by tier, FP/PL/MK researched For arguments (footprint, politics, markets), OW/FR further wins and frames.

If something breaks

  • The header dot shows sync. Green means shared and live. Red means offline: keep logging, entries queue and send when the connection returns.
  • "Keyword only" means no model is available. Matching still works, in keyword order. Haiku needs an API key under Session on this hosted version; the key stays on your device.
  • Anyone who knows the session code can read and add to the log, so use a fresh code for the real round.
