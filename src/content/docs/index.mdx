<div class="hero">

I'm a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Toronto. I build electromechanical hardware and the analysis that justifies it: a machined copper condenser and test rig for a wickless vapor chamber at the Centre for Advanced Coating Technologies, a 3D-printed audio record and turntable designed from FDM resolution and stylus contact limits, a compliant tendon-driven finger joint for a rehabilitation glove, an optimized 3D-printed gearbox, a wearable tremor stabilizer built in 48 hours, and a custom biopotential acquisition PCB. Most recently I reverse-engineered a chip from its GDS layout.

I work from physical constraints first: what the process can resolve, what the material can survive, and what a measurement can actually confirm. Each writeup below states the problem, the numbers that drove the design, what was built and tested, and what I would do next. My interests sit at the intersection of mechatronics, precision mechanisms, and the manufacturing processes that limit them.

## Projects

<CardGrid>

  <a href="/EngineeringPortfolio/projects/cact">
    <Card title="CACT: copper condenser for a wickless vapor chamber">
      Machined a C110 condenser to a GD&T drawing (NPT ports, deep thermocouple bores, sealing face flatness), calibrated 14 thermocouples on a DAQ, ran the first heater-to-chiller test.

  <a href="/EngineeringPortfolio/projects/openvinyl">
    <Card title="OpenVinyl: 3D-printed audio records and turntable">
      v1 hill-and-dale record printed and played. v2 cuts the groove sideways by writing the printer's G-code directly, with feedrate scheduled from the audio. Every parameter derived from bead width, stylus contact and printer acceleration.
    </Card>
  </a>

  <a href="/EngineeringPortfolio/projects/poststrokerehab">
    <Card title="Compliant tendon-driven finger joint (NeuroTech UofT)">
      Parametric Onshape flexure array validated with nonlinear displacement-controlled FEA to 90° and printed in PLA. Restoring torque sized against the estimated Bowden and joint friction floor.
    </Card>
  </a>

  <a href="/EngineeringPortfolio/projects/gearbox">
    <Card title="Optimized 3D-printed 3:1 speed reducer">
      Mixed-integer gear train optimization (differential evolution, AGMA stresses adapted for PLA), involute CAD, printed and assembled with photo instructions.
    </Card>
  </a>

  <a href="/EngineeringPortfolio/projects/asicpuzzle2026">
    <Card title="Reverse engineering an ASIC from its GDS layout">

    </Card>
  </a>

  <a href="/EngineeringPortfolio/projects/asicpuzzle2026-starbattle">
